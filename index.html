About Neer Arts & Joy
NA

A small studio that began with a few colours and one big feeling

I started Neer Arts & Joy because I kept noticing that the gifts people remember are never the expensive ones. They are the ones that carry a memory. As a child I used to paint little cards for my aunt on her birthday, and she kept every one of them in a steel trunk for years.

That trunk stayed with me. Today, from Bhopal, I make that kind of gift for other families: a wedding bouquet turned into a frame, a baby's first handprint, a tray painted for a daughter's new home.

Every order starts with a conversation, not a catalogue. You tell me the story, and I put it into colour.

Founder, Neer Arts & Joy
